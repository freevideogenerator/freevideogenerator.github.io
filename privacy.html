Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FreeVideoGenerator ("we", "us", "our") at freevideogenerator.github.io collects, uses and protects information when you use our website. By using the website you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information – if you create an account, we receive your name, email address and, for Google sign-in, your profile photo. Accounts are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password.
  • Prompts and uploads – the text prompts, reference images and audio you provide are sent directly from your browser to the AI service that generates your result. We do not store them on our own servers.
  • Generated content – images and videos are created and kept in your browser session. They are not saved by us; download anything you want to keep.
  • Local settings – optional tokens (such as a Hugging Face token) and preferences are stored only in your browser's local storage and are never sent to us.
  • Usage data – like most websites, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and advertising partners may automatically collect technical data such as IP address, browser type, device and pages visited.

2. Third-party AI services

To generate content, your prompt and images are processed by third-party services, including Hugging Face Spaces (Z-Image, FLUX, Qwen-Image, LTX-Video, Wan, MMAudio, text-to-speech), Pollinations.ai and, if you choose them, Puter (Nano Banana / Google Gemini and Veo). These services have their own privacy policies and may log requests. Please do not upload private or sensitive photos of other people without their consent.

3. Cookies and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show advertisements. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Firebase Authentication also uses browser storage to keep you signed in. You can clear cookies and site data at any time in your browser settings.

4. How we use information

  • To let you sign in and use the studio.
  • To send account emails such as verification and password reset.
  • To keep the website secure, prevent abuse and improve features.
  • To display advertising that keeps the service free.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your account stays active until you ask us to delete it. To delete your account and associated data, email creatorvoicegenerator@gmail.com from the address linked to your account.

6. Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to withdraw consent. Contact us to make a request.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.
