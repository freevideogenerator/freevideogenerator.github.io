About Us

FreeVideoGenerator is a free online AI studio that helps creators turn ideas into images and short videos — in Hindi and English. Our goal is simple: anyone, from a village YouTuber to a small business, should be able to make good-looking video content without paying for expensive software.

हमारा मकसद है कि हर क्रिएटर बिना पैसे खर्च किए अपनी कहानी, रील या YouTube वीडियो AI की मदद से बना सके।

What you can do

  • Text to Image / Characters – create images and consistent characters from a prompt with free open models such as Z-Image Turbo, FLUX.1 Schnell and Qwen-Image, or with Nano Banana using reference photos.
  • Text to Video – describe a scene and get a short clip, with AI sound effects and Hindi/English voice.
  • Ingredients to Video – give characters, places or objects as reference images and place them in a video.
  • Frames to Video – set a first (and last) frame and let AI create the motion in between.
  • Scene Builder – join several scenes, add a caption and music, and download one MP4 for Reels or YouTube.

How it is free

The studio runs on free and open AI models hosted on public infrastructure such as Hugging Face ZeroGPU and Pollinations, plus optional providers like Puter for Nano Banana and Veo. Free services have daily limits; when one is busy, the site automatically tries the next free model. The website is supported by advertising.

Who we are

FreeVideoGenerator is an independent project built in India for Hindi-speaking creators. We are not affiliated with Google, Hugging Face, Alibaba, Black Forest Labs, Lightricks or any other model provider; their names are used only to describe which model generated your content.
